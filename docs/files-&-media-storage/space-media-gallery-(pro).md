# Space Media Gallery (Pro)

FluentCommunity automatically gathers every photo and video shared inside a Space and displays them together in one browsable **Media Gallery**. Instead of scrolling through the feed to find that image someone posted last week, your members can open the gallery and see all of a Space's visual content in a single, organized grid.

This guide explains how to enable, configure, and use the Media Gallery for a Space.

::: tip
The Media Gallery requires the [FluentCommunity Pro version](/installation-&-activation) installed and activated.
:::

## How the Media Gallery Works

The Media Gallery is **automatic**. There is no separate "upload to gallery" step — any photo or video shared in a Space's posts is collected into that Space's gallery for you. The gallery simply gives your members a cleaner way to browse media that already lives in the Space.

## Enable the Media Gallery

Go to the **Spaces** section from the FluentCommunity navbar. Open the Space where you want the gallery, click the **three-dot menu** in the top-right corner, and select [**Space Settings**](/space-settings-&-privacy). Scroll down to the **Permissions** section.

**Enable Media Gallery:** Find the **Enable Media Gallery for this space** option — marked with a **(Pro Feature)** badge — and check the box to turn the gallery on.

The Media Gallery is **off by default**. You turn it on per Space, exactly like [the File & Document Library](/the-file-&-document-library-(pro)).

**Media Gallery Access (View):** Once enabled, choose who is allowed to view the gallery under **Media Gallery Access (View)**:

* **Members of this space** — only members of the Space can open the gallery.
* **Any Logged in users** — any logged-in user of your site can view it.
* **Public** — anyone can view it, even without logging in.

After configuring, scroll down and click the **Update Info** button to save your changes.

::: info Privacy note
Private and secret Spaces always keep their gallery restricted to members, regardless of the access option you pick — the gallery never exposes media from a hidden Space.
:::

![The Enable Media Gallery option and its access control in Space Settings](/images/files-media-storage/space-media-gallery/enable-media-gallery.webp)

## Viewing the Media Gallery

Once enabled, open the Space and go to the **Media Gallery** page. At the top you'll find two tabs:

* **Photos** — every image shared in the Space, shown as a responsive thumbnail grid.
* **Videos** — every video shared in the Space, each shown with a preview thumbnail and a **play badge** so videos are easy to spot.

![The Media Gallery page showing the Photos and Videos tabs](/images/files-media-storage/space-media-gallery/photos-and-videos-tabs.webp)

## Opening Photos and Videos

**Photos:** Click any photo to open it full-screen in a lightbox viewer. When a Space has several photos, the viewer shows a position counter (for example, *1 / 8*) so you always know where you are as you page through them.

**Videos:** Click any video to open the video lightbox. From there you can:

* **Play** the video right inside the lightbox.
* Jump to the **previous** or **next** video using the arrow buttons.
* See the video's **title**, its **date**, and a **position counter** in the meta bar.
* **Close** the lightbox to return to the grid.

The gallery is fully keyboard-accessible — you can move between items with the keyboard and open any photo or video with <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>.

## If You Don't Have Access

If a member opens a gallery they aren't allowed to view, FluentCommunity shows a clear **"Permission Denied"** message with the note *"You do not have permission to view this page"*, plus a **Go to Space Home** button to send them back to the Space.

The Media Gallery gives every Space a polished, automatic home for its shared photos and videos — no manual uploading, no extra work, and full control over who can see it.
