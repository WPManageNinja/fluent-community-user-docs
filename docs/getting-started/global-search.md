# Global Search & Command Palette

Global Search allows you to find anything in your community from one box. You can search posts, comments, people, spaces, courses and lessons together, or jump straight to a page without clicking through menus.

> **Use Case:** A member remembers a helpful reply from last month but not which space it was in. They press **Ctrl+K**, type a few words, and the matching posts and comments appear in one list.

## Open the Search Box

You can open search in two ways:

* Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows and Linux) anywhere in the portal.
* Click the **Search...** box in the header bar.

![Screenshot of the Search box in the portal header, with a ⌘K badge](/images/getting-started/global-search/global-search-box.webp)

## Jump to a Page

Before you type anything, the box shows quick links grouped under **Jump to**, **Your Spaces** and **Actions**.

* **Jump to** opens main pages such as **Feed**, **Spaces**, **Courses**, **Members** and **Bookmarks**.
* **Your Spaces** lists the spaces you have joined.
* **Actions** has shortcuts such as **Edit my profile**, **Notification settings** and **Switch to dark mode**.

Use the **Up** and **Down** arrow keys to move through the list, **Enter** to open the highlighted item, and **Esc** to close the box.

![Screenshot of the search box before typing, listing Jump to pages, Your Spaces and Actions](/images/getting-started/global-search/global-search-jump-to.webp)


## Search Your Community

Type a word or phrase to search. Results are grouped by type, and the words you typed are highlighted in each result.

The **All** tab shows a few results of each type. Posts, comments, people, spaces, courses and individual lessons can all appear. Select **See all posts** to see more.

::: info Members only see what they can access
Search shows each person only the results they already have access to. Posts from a private space do not appear for someone who has not joined it.
:::

![Screenshot of search results for "welcome", with tabs for All, Posts, Comments, People, Spaces and Courses, and a Sort menu](/images/getting-started/global-search/global-search-results.webp)



### Narrow Down Results

Click a tab to see only one type of result.

* **Posts** shows matching posts, each with its space, author, time and comment count.
* **Comments** shows matching comments.
* **People**, **Spaces** and **Courses** show members, spaces and courses.

Use the **Sort** menu to change the order of post results. **Relevance** is the default. You can also sort by newest or by most popular.

![Screenshot of the Posts tab, listing only posts that match the search](/images/getting-started/global-search/global-search-posts-tab.webp)


## Use Shortcuts

The bar at the bottom of the box lists three shortcuts. Type the symbol first, then keep typing.

| Type | What it does |
|---|---|
| `>` | Shows **Actions** and **Go to** pages, so you can run a command or open a page. |
| `#` | Lists your spaces. Type a name after it to find one. |
| `@` | Searches for people. Type a name to find a member. |


::: tip No results?
If a search finds nothing, the box suggests trying a different search, such as **Search in comments**.
:::
