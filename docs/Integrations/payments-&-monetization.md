# Payments & Monetization

FluentCommunity does not process payments on its own. Instead, it connects to a dedicated payment plugin, which handles the checkout, the transaction, and any ongoing subscription. FluentCommunity then listens for a successful payment and grants the buyer access to the Space or Course you linked to that product.

This separation means you keep full control of your store, your tax settings, and your payment gateways, while enrolment stays automatic.

### What You Will Learn

In the following articles, we will explore the two supported payment integrations:

* **Connecting with FluentCart:** Sell access to your Courses and Spaces through a full-featured store, with product management, order history, and reporting built in.
* **Connecting with Paymattic:** Monetize your community using flexible payment and subscription forms, a good fit if you want a lighter setup than a full store.

::: tip
Both integrations support one-time payments and recurring subscriptions. If you are deciding between them, choose FluentCart when you want a complete store experience, and Paymattic when you mainly need payment forms.
:::
