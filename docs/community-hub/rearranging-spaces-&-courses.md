# Re-arranging Spaces & Courses

By default, your spaces and courses listings decide their own order. With **Re-arrange**, admins put them in the order members should meet them, rather than the order they were created.

> **Use Case:** Your onboarding course and the **Start Here** space are the oldest items on the site, but you want new members to see them first. Drag them to the top, save, and lock the sort so everyone sees your order.

## Re-arranging the Order

1. Open the **Spaces** (discover) page or the **Courses** page in your community portal.
2. Open the page menu and click **Re-arrange**.

![The Re-arrange option in the Spaces page menu](/images/community-hub/rearranging-spaces-&-courses/rearrange-menu.webp)

3. In the drawer, drag each space or course into the position you want. You stay on the page, so there is no reload.

> [!Note] 
> New spaces and courses no longer sink below your custom order. Secret spaces appear in the drawer for the people who are allowed to see them.

![The Re-arrange drawer with spaces being dragged into a custom order](/images/community-hub/rearranging-spaces-&-courses/rearrange-drawer.webp)


## Choosing the Default Sort

Two settings in **Customization Settings** control how members see each listing:

1. Go to **Portal Settings** and open **Customization**.
2. Find the sort settings for **Spaces** and **Courses**.
3. Pick the **default sort** members see on each listing.
4. To show everyone the same thing, turn the sort dropdown **off**, so your custom order is the only order.
5. Click **Save Settings**.

> **Tip:** To use your drag-and-drop order as the order members see, choose the custom order as the default sort and turn the sort dropdown off.

![The default sort and sort dropdown settings for Spaces and Courses](/images/community-hub/rearranging-spaces-&-courses/sort-settings.webp)
