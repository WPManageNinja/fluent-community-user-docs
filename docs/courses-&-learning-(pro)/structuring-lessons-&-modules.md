# Structuring Lessons & Modules

Once you have created a course, the next step is to add the learning content. FluentCommunity uses a flexible structure of **Sections** and **Lessons** to help you organize your course material in a logical and easy-to-follow way.

* **Sections** are like chapters or modules. They are used to group related lessons together.
* **Lessons** are the individual pages of content, such as a video, a piece of text, or a downloadable worksheet.

> **Use Case:** For a course on "Website Design," you could create a "Module 1: Introduction to HTML" section, which contains individual lessons like "Lesson 1: Basic Page Structure" and "Lesson 2: Working with Text & Links."

## Accessing the Course Structure Editor

1.  Navigate to **Portal Settings → Course Management → Manage Courses**.
2.  Click on the course you want to edit.
3.  On the course screen, click the **Lessons** tab (next to **Overview** and **Students**).

This opens the Lessons screen for that course, showing its Type, Status, and Visibility, along with a running count of sections and lessons (e.g., "5 Sections • 0 Lesson").

![The Lessons screen showing a course's sections](/images/course-and-learning/structure-course/course-builder-1.webp)


## Step 1: Create a New Section

First, you need to create a section to hold your lessons.

1.  Click the **+ Add Section** button in the top-right corner of the Lessons panel.
2.  In the **Section Title** field that appears, enter a name for the section (e.g., "Welcome & Introduction").
3.  Click **Save**.

Your new section will appear in the list, with a drag handle on the left for reordering it later.

![The Add Section popup with the Section Title field](/images/course-and-learning/structure-course/section-title-2.webp)

## Step 2: Add a Lesson to the Section

Now you can add individual lessons inside the section.

1.  Click the **Add New** button on the right-hand side of the section row.
2.  In the **Lesson Title** field, enter a title for the lesson (e.g., "Meet Your Instructor").
3.  Click **Add** to create the lesson and stay on the Lessons screen, or **Add & Edit** to create it and jump straight into the lesson editor.

![The Add Lesson popup with the Lesson Title field, Add and Add & Edit buttons](/images/course-and-learning/structure-course/lesson-title-3.webp)

Once a section has lessons, its row shows a lesson-count pill (e.g., "1 lesson") you can click to expand or collapse the list, and each lesson underneath shows a status badge (**Published** or **Draft**) plus its own three-dot menu.


#### Edit a Lesson Slug (Optional)

Each lesson gets a URL slug generated from its title, and you can change it at any time to something cleaner or more descriptive.

1.  Click the **three-dot menu** next to the lesson and select **Edit Slug**.


![The lesson three-dot menu showing Edit Content, Edit Title, Edit Slug, and more](/images/course-and-learning/structure-course/edit-slug-4.webp)


2.  In the **Slug (optional)** field, enter your preferred URL-friendly text.
3.  Click the **Save** button. The change applies immediately.

> [!Tip]
> Courses have their own **Slug** field on the course details screen see [Creating Your First Course](/creating-your-first-course). Keeping lesson slugs short and descriptive makes shared lesson links much easier to read.

![The Slug popup, pre-filled with a URL-friendly slug](/images/course-and-learning/structure-course/edit-slug-5.webp)

## Step 3: Add Content to Your Lesson

Choosing **Add & Edit**, or **Edit Content** from a lesson's three-dot menu, opens FluentCommunity's built-in lesson editor, a block-based editor similar in spirit to the WordPress block editor (Gutenberg), letting you build the lesson from individual content blocks.

* Add paragraphs of text for written instruction.
* Embed videos from services like YouTube or Vimeo, or upload your own.
* Add images, galleries, and downloadable files.
* Include lists, quotes, and other rich formatting blocks via the **Type `/` to choose a block** prompt.

The top toolbar shows which lesson you're on (e.g., "Lesson 1 of 1"), with arrows to jump to the previous or next lesson without leaving the editor, plus the lesson's **Published**/Draft status and a **Save** button. 

Selecting a block opens a settings panel on the right with **Typography**, **Background**, **Dimensions**, **Border**, **Elements**, and **Advanced** controls for fine-tuning its appearance.

Once you are finished adding content to your lesson, click **Save**, then **Back to Lessons** in the top-left corner to return to the course structure and add more lessons or sections.
